# Glamping with jacuzzi near Medellín: from domes to country luxury suites

The dream of an Andean escape to **glamping with a jacuzzi near Medellín** immediately brings seductive imagery to mind: steaming outdoor water, an uncorked bottle of red wine, warm lanterns, and mountain ridges etched against an amber sunset.

Yet behind carefully filtered social media photos, the reality of standard dome glamping often falls short: PVC geodesic domes that turn into sweltering greenhouses by noon, damp midnight chills with condensation dripping from the ceiling, and paper-thin walls carrying every conversation from neighboring tents.

For travelers seeking genuine immersion in nature without sacrificing comfort, an essential upgrade exists: stepping from cramped plastic domes into a private luxury countryside suite with an outdoor jacuzzi.

---

## 1. The everyday shortcomings of canvas and plastic domes

The sudden explosion of geodesic domes in chilly highland areas like Guatapé or Santa Elena introduced many to nature getaways, but also revealed persistent design flaws:

- **Midday greenhouse effect:** Clear vinyl shells trap solar radiation. Between 11:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m., dome interiors often become uncomfortably hot.
- **Nighttime dampness:** Trapped human respiration against cold vinyl creates moisture droplets that dampen luggage and bedcovers.
- **Acoustic transparency:** Fabric walls cannot block sound. Music, chatter, or roadway engines intrude upon intimate moments.
- **Cramped modular bathrooms:** Tight prefabricated stalls with low-pressure showerheads and point-of-use heaters that struggle to maintain warm water.

## 2. Countryside suites: noble architecture and thermal serenity

Against the fragility of vinyl, the traditional architecture of southwestern Antioquia delivers a lasting answer. Building with noble regional materials—hand-hewn timber, clay tile roofs, and thick insulated walls—creates spaces that breathe in harmony with the climate:

- **Bioclimatic insulation:** Walls absorb daytime warmth and release it gently after dark, maintaining a steady, comfortable temperature of 22°C to 24°C (72°F to 75°F) without loud air conditioning.
- **King and Queen hotel-grade bedding:** High-support mattresses, premium high-thread-count cotton linens, and warm duvets ensure restorative sleep.
- **Spacious en-suite bathrooms:** Natural stone finishes, steady high-pressure rain showers, and abundant natural daylight.

## 3. The private jacuzzi ritual facing Cerro Tusa

The true centerpiece of any romantic or restorative retreat is the outdoor wet area. At La Juana, the jacuzzi is not an acrylic tub dropped onto a wooden deck; it is a custom hydromassage installation crafted in natural stone with a front-row view of the world's largest natural pyramid.

Picture the ritual:
1. By 5:30 p.m., our butler warms the jacuzzi to a soothing 38°C (100°F).
2. Mountain breezes descend from the high cordillera while therapeutic jets soothe tired back muscles.
3. The horizon glows in violet, gold, and crimson as the sun dips behind the razor-sharp pyramid crest.
4. Two glasses of wine on the deck, unhurried by checkout hours or other guests waiting their turn, beneath a sky brimming with constellations.

## 4. Catered home dining: the joy of never cooking

A common hassle of isolated glamping sites is dining: cold picnic hampers, delayed delivery orders from town, or cooking on portable camping stoves.

At La Juana, your stay includes two dedicated private cooks who turn daily meals into effortless indulgence:
