# Things to do with kids in Antioquia: outdoor adventures and family getaways

When school breaks or holiday weekends arrive, city routines in Medellín often trap families in repetitive habits: crowded indoor malls, noisy gaming arcades, or movie theaters. Yet just a short drive from the valley lies an expansive landscape of emerald Andean mountains, rushing clean streams, and historic mule trails where childhood flourishes naturally.

Finding **things to do with kids in Antioquia** that entertain energetic children while offering genuine relaxation for parents requires choosing destinations with smooth roads, pleasant weather, and enriching activities. In southwestern Antioquia, families discover this balance effortlessly.

---

## 1. Why nature is the ultimate children's playground

Childhood development specialists consistently affirm what grandparents knew by heart: direct, open-ended contact with soil, trees, and animals improves focus, reduces screen-related stress, and strengthens emotional resilience.

In a rural Colombian environment:
- **Play is Imaginative and Free:** A fallen branch becomes a wizard's staff, river pebbles become sailing boats, and shady coffee groves become secret kingdoms.
- **Sensory Engagement:** Feeling cool dew on bare feet, hearing dawn bird calls, and tasting fresh tropical fruit plucked from orchard trees enlivens young senses.
- **Deeper Family Bonds:** Splashing together in the pool or gathering around an evening fire pit creates indelible memories that outlast store-bought toys.

## 2. Four top family adventures in the Suroeste

Less than two and a half hours from Medellín via modern 4G highways, Venecia and its valleys feature activities designed to delight younger travelers:

### Adventure 1: Discoveries at Parque Comfama Cerro Tusa
Located just five minutes from La Juana, this vast ecological reserve offers well-graded trails winding alongside crystal streams, shady picnic groves beneath ancient fig trees, and interactive educational stations explaining native fauna and Zenúfana pre-Columbian culture.

### Adventure 2: The Stingless «Angelita» Bee Tour (Educational & Safe)
Organized in partnership with local guides at **Al Parche Venecia**, this family tour introduces children to native stingless bees. Equipped with pint-sized explorer suits, kids can safely observe hives without any fear of stings, learn about pollination, and sample fresh medicinal honey straight from the comb.

### Adventure 3: Gentle Trail Rides Through Coffee Groves
Gentle Colombian criollo horses are patient and well-accustomed to novice young riders. Led by local wranglers, children experience the thrill of riding through hillside coffee groves, feeling like authentic mountain arrieros.

### Adventure 4: Viewing the Natural Pyramid
While climbing to the razor-sharp peak of [the largest natural pyramid in the world](/en/blog/cerro-tusa/largest-natural-pyramid-in-the-world) is reserved for teens and adults due to steep slopes, the gentle hike to its base viewpoint is perfect for children aged 7 and up, ending with a triumphant family photo.

## Child Safety and Health Essentials

- **Constant Pool Supervision:** Even in shallow areas, an adult must always accompany young swimmers.
- **Continuous Hydration:** With mountain sunshine and energetic play, keep fresh water and tropical juices easily accessible.
- **Basic Family First Aid:** Pack pediatric antihistamines, a thermometer, and bandages for minor scrapes.

## Practical 48-Hour Weekend Family Itinerary

A realistic, kid-tested weekend schedule balancing adventure, pool time, and restorative downtime for parents:

- **Day 1 - Morning Departure & Pool Fun:** Depart Medellín at 7:30 a.m. to arrive at La Juana by 10:00 a.m. Welcome fresh juices and immediate transition to the swimming pool while the house staff unpacks groceries and sets up rooms.
- **Day 1 - Afternoon Games & Sunset Bonfire:** Casual family lunch on the terrace followed by outdoor lawn games and board games in the shaded kiosk. Dusk hot tub soak followed by a campfire under the stars with hot cocoa and roasted marshmallows.
- **Day 2 - Comfama Nature Exploration:** Short 5-minute drive to Parque Comfama Cerro Tusa after breakfast. Children love the rope-climbing playground and interactive indigenous exhibits. Return to the estate for a hearty Sunday lunch and a leisurely afternoon swim before driving back to Medellín.
