# Finca La Juana: a luxury experience at the foot of Cerro Tusa

The first thing that hits you at La Juana is not what you see. It is what you stop hearing.

The noise of the city faded two curves ago. The phone stopped buzzing one curve before that. And when the car pulls up in front of the bahareque facade with its clay tile roof, the only presence left is the silence of the mountain. Behind it all, the shape of Cerro Tusa cuts against the sky.

Here, where the clouds hug the coffee farms and time slows down to honor old roots, is La Juana. One of the [luxury farmhouses in Colombia](/en/blog/luxury-fincas) that understands luxury as silence.

---

## The story of La Juana

La Juana started from an idea that sounds simple but gets harder to find every year: bring back the old hospitality of Antioquia and put it at the service of people who want more than a bed for the night.

It sits in Venecia, Antioquia, two and a half hours from Medellín, in the Rochiles parcel. A short walk from Parque Comfama Cerro Tusa. Far enough that the only sound you hear is the wind moving through the coffee plants.

The farmhouse is a tribute to traditional architecture: bahareque, fine woods, clay tile, wide corridors that ask you to sit down without rushing. Inside, though, every detail speaks of modern comfort. Of a luxury that does not need to show off.

It sits in the Rochiles parcel, Venecia — for those planning [things to do in Venecia Antioquia](/en/blog/cerro-tusa/things-to-do-venecia-antioquia), the farmhouse is the starting point.

## Living the farmhouse: six suites, one experience

La Juana rents out as a whole. You do not share spaces with strangers, no guests crossing paths in the hallway. Six rooms, eight beds, six bathrooms. Room for sixteen people who come to live the farmhouse as if it were their own.

Each suite has its own character. Some look to the garden, others to the mountain. All of them have bedding that invites you to stay in bed until late. The coffee from Diamante, grown on the slopes of the region, arrives every morning with the exact aroma to start the day.

The pool is not decorative. It gets used. The private jacuzzi looks out over the mountain. The steam room is for cold afternoons when the fog rolls down and the only sensible thing is to surrender to the heat. The bonfire, at nightfall, becomes the natural meeting point.

There is fast WiFi for whoever needs it. There are also corners where the phone loses signal, for whoever prefers not to need it.

## The service that makes the difference

What sets La Juana apart from other luxury farmhouses in Antioquia has as much to do with the landscape as with the team that welcomes the guests.

Two maids and a butler are included in every booking. Not as an optional extra. They are part of the experience: people from the region who know the farmhouse, the food, the trails. They cook, they attend, they recommend. And they make every group — a family, a corporate team, a group of friends — feel looked after without feeling watched.

That care is what has taken La Juana to a **4.95-star rating**. A number that, in hospitality, is almost a myth.

## In front of the Great Natural Pyramid

Cerro Tusa is the world's largest natural pyramid: a volcanic formation 1,950 meters above sea level. The Zenúfanas, the pre-Columbian people who lived in this region, considered it a sacred place.

From La Juana's terrace, the mountain is fully visible. It imposes. It changes color with the hours: deep green in the morning, blue-gray at midday, golden when the sun sets behind it.

Guests can climb it with guides from Parque Comfama Cerro Tusa. The entrance is five minutes from the farmhouse. The hike takes four to five hours and passes artificial terraces of indigenous origin, stone altars, caves with traces of ritual use. At the top, the view is 360 degrees: all of Antioquia looks like a map laid out flat.

Not everyone climbs. Some prefer to watch it from the pool, a book in hand and a cup of Diamante coffee steaming beside them. That is also a way of living it. For those who prefer comfort near the pyramid without giving up nature, [glamping at Cerro Tusa](/en/blog/glamping/glamping-cerro-tusa) is the alternative.

## Experiences for those who want more

La Juana has built alliances with local operators to offer experiences that go beyond the stay:

- **Cerro Tusa tour** — A guided climb of advanced level, with Zenúfana history and panoramic views.
- **The bees tour** — A family experience where guests put on the beekeeper suit, visit the hives and taste pure honey from the region.
- **Cultural walk through Venecia** — The Street of Murals, the Cerro Tusa Zenufaná Museum, and the House Museum of master Carlos Maldonado, a local sculptor who carves in cement and sand.

And for those who do not want to arrange anything, there is the complete plan: the Stay & Climb "The Mirror Goddess Retreat", two days with ascent, pool, night bonfire, sunrise yoga and local food. Or the Day Tour "The Sacred Ascent", a 12-hour immersion from Medellín that includes the climb, a typical lunch at Graciela's restaurant at the foot of the mountain, and an afternoon of pool and massage with a physiotherapist at La Juana.
