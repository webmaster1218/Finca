La Juana Cerro Tusa is that place.

## Why choose an event venue in Antioquia?

Antioquia has a healthy obsession with celebrations. We, the Antioqueños, know that an event is not just about gathering people: it is about building the stage where memorable things happen. A **finca for large groups** offers what no closed ballroom can. Kids can run. Adults can talk without raising their voices. The music plays without bothering a single neighbor.

At La Juana, that experience multiplies. The grounds are laid out so guests never feel crowded or lost. There are separate zones for eating, dancing, resting and exploring, all connected by dirt paths and gardens that invite you to walk barefoot.

### What a finca gives you that a ballroom can't:

- **Real air and real space.** No air conditioning. Kids run, adults breathe deep, and the celebration feels alive.
- **A sunset that cannot be rented.** As guests arrive, the sun drops behind Cerro Tusa. No amount of decoration buys that.
- **Total flexibility.** Want guests to arrive on Friday? Keep the party going until morning? On a finca, time bends to your plan, not the other way around.
- **Privacy.** No front desk, no other events crossing paths in the hallways. The finca is yours for the celebration.

Every celebration deserves its own natural stage. The finca offers several environments, each ready for a different moment of the event.

### The main garden — for the ceremony or the toast

An open space with a direct view of Cerro Tusa. Well-kept lawn, regional flowers and enough natural shade to keep guests comfortable. This is where we set up the altar, the dance floor or just the long tables for a countryside lunch.

## Catering: the Antioquian flavor your guests will remember

A celebration without good food is just a meeting. At La Juana, catering is designed around each event and the taste of the guests.

**Options available:**

- **Traditional Antioquian menu:** bandeja paisa, chicken sancocho, mondongo, chócolo arepas with butter and hogao.
- **Grill option:** aged meats, Antioquian chorizo, morcilla, salted potatoes, guacamole.
- **Light option (ideal for retreats):** fresh salads, grilled proteins, vegetarian options, natural juices from the region.
- **Cocktails and appetizers:** to welcome guests as they arrive, with local flavors like coconut lemonade, guarapo, empanadas and cheese sticks.

Want to bring your own caterer? That works too. The finca provides the kitchen setup so your provider can work comfortably, or you can book the service we recommend directly: local cooks from the vereda who know the craft by heart.

The wedding that starts as guests arrive while the sun hides behind Cerro Tusa. **Best for:** companies of 20 to 40 people that want to take the team out of the routine without going too far from Medellín. For a lighter plan, there is the [day-by-the-pool option](/en/blog/farm-stays-colombia/day-by-the-pool-antioquia).

## Frequently asked questions about events at La Juana

### How long does the rental last for an event?

It depends on the celebration. For weddings and birthdays, the typical window is 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Corporate retreats can run from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Overnight stays have check-in at 3:00 p.m. and check-out at 12:00 p.m.

### Is music and sound allowed?

Yes. The finca has basic sound equipment, and you can bring your own amplification. Music is allowed until 10:00 p.m., out of respect for the neighbors of the vereda.

### Is there parking for guests?

Yes. The finca has parking for around 15 vehicles at the entrance. There is an extended parking option for large events, with prior coordination.

### Are decorations and setups allowed?

Yes. You can bring your own decorator or do it yourself. We only ask that you do not nail anything to the trees and avoid materials that leave residue on the grass. Trash is separated and disposed of responsibly.

### What if it rains? Is there a plan B?

Antioquia is unpredictable. La Juana has covered zones, including the main terrace, the covered dining area and an indoor hall, that work as backup without the event losing its essence.
