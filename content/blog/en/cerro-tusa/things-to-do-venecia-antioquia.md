# Things to Do in Venecia Antioquia: A Complete Guide

Venecia Antioquia is one of those towns that does not announce itself. It just appears. You come across it when you least expect it, two hours from Medellín, and by the time you arrive you understand why the people who know it keep coming back.

It sits at 1,457 meters above sea level, wrapped in green mountains that change shade with every hour of the day. There is a hill that looks hand-carved, Cerro Tusa, the world's largest natural pyramid, and a town center that keeps the old Antioquia style of wooden balconies and cobblestone streets.

If you are looking for things to do in Venecia Antioquia, this is what you need to know.

---

## Things to Do in Venecia Antioquia: Must-See Attractions

### 1. Cerro Tusa tour, the great natural pyramid

Cerro Tusa is the reason many people make the trip to Venecia Antioquia. At 1,950 meters above sea level with 600 meters of prominence from the base, it is the largest natural pyramid on the planet, a volcanic formation that the pre-Hispanic peoples of the region held as sacred.

The guided tour, run by **Al Parche Venecia**, lasts about five hours. It is rated level 5, advanced: the climb is demanding, with steep stretches and rocky ground. Skip it if you are a beginner hiker. The view from the top, with the whole southwest of Antioquia laid out like a map, makes every step worth it.

**It includes:** a specialized guide, insurance, and a stop at the Altar of Ofrendas, an archeological lookout with pre-Hispanic remains.

**Tip:** leave early, around 6:30 to 7 am. The summit clears of clouds between 9 and 10 am, and the midday heat makes the descent harder.

![Hiking trail up Cerro Tusa in Venecia Antioquia, between green mountains and tropical vegetation](/blog/que-hacer-venecia-antioquia/cuerpo-1.webp)

For those who prefer to let someone else handle the whole day, there is a ready-made option: the [Sacred Ascent tour](/en/tours/sacred-ascent), a twelve-hour round trip from Medellín.

### 2. The bees tour, an apiculture experience

This one surprises people. You put on the beekeeper suit, walk into the hives, watch how the bees work, and taste pure honey pulled fresh. The smell of warm wax mixed with mountain air stays with you.

It lasts four hours and works for the whole family, even for people who are afraid of bees. The suit covers you completely and the hives are handled by experts.

![Bee tour in Venecia Antioquia, wooden hives in a green valley with a beekeeper in a white suit](/blog/que-hacer-venecia-antioquia/cuerpo-2.webp)

### 3. A walk through the old town

Venecia keeps a traditional Antioquia center that deserves a slow walk. The **Street of Murals** is painted with work by local artists telling the story of the town. The main church, with its white facade and simple bell tower, overlooks the park.

Two small but genuine museums are worth a stop, one on local history and one on religious art. Either one takes thirty to forty minutes.

**Do not miss:** the park café in the afternoon. Locals sit there and watch the light change over the mountains. The coffee is regional, and the coffee farms you see on the hillsides grow specialty beans.

### 4. Waterfalls and nature

Around Venecia Antioquia there are several waterfalls you can reach on your own. They are not as well known as the ones in nearby towns, and that is exactly their advantage. You arrive and you are alone with the sound of the water.

The most recommended:
- **El Chispero waterfall**, twenty minutes from town, reached by a horse trail
- **Charco del Amor**, a natural pool in the river, good for a swim on sunny days
- **Quebrada La Liboriana**, crystal waters surrounded by thick vegetation

Ask in town for the current routes. Locals know the trails and which ones are in the best shape depending on the season.

## Where is Venecia Antioquia and how to get there

Venecia Antioquia sits in the southwest of the department, 68 km from Medellín. Getting there is straightforward: the drive takes between one hour 45 minutes and two hours 15 minutes depending on traffic.

**Driving from Medellín:**
1. Take the Autopista Sur toward the La Pintada / Santa Bárbara exit
2. Pass through Caldas and Santa Bárbara, then continue on the road that climbs toward Venecia
3. Turn left at the La Pintada toll, following clear signs

The road is fully paved. The last stretch, after Santa Bárbara, is sharp curves between coffee farms. The route itself is straightforward. Still, drive it in daylight to enjoy the landscape.

**By bus:** if you prefer not to drive, buses from Transportes Venecia leave the Terminal del Sur in Medellín every 30 to 45 minutes. The fare is about $18,000 COP and the trip lasts two hours 15 minutes.

**Exact coordinates:** 5°58′N 75°44′W

## Climate in Venecia Antioquia: what to expect

The climate in Venecia Antioquia is mild mountain weather. The average temperature stays between 18°C and 24°C all year.

There is no marked dry season. It rains often, especially between April and May and between October and November. But the rain is usually short, a twenty-minute downpour followed by bright sun that lights up the mountains in a green that looks brand new.

**What to pack:** a light jacket for the night, since the temperature drops to 16-18°C after 6 pm, comfortable walking shoes, and layers you can take off and put on. A waterproof jacket never hurts.

**Best time to visit:** December to March and July to August see the least rain. But Venecia has a special charm under the drizzle, when the clouds come down to touch the rooftops and Cerro Tusa shows itself through the mist.

## Where to eat in Venecia Antioquia

The food scene in Venecia is modest but honest. Family restaurants that cook like home:

- **Restaurante El Parque**, across from the church. Bandeja paisa, chicken sancocho, beans. Nothing fancy, all of it tasty.
- **La Terraza de Venecia**, with valley views. Try the trout fillet with patacones. They have micheladas on weekends.
- **Panadería El Buen Pan**, homemade desserts, pan de yuca, empanadas that burst with hot broth. Perfect for a snack before the Cerro Tusa tour.

If you are staying at a farmhouse with a kitchen service, that is the best option, especially when breakfast includes arepa de maíz pelado, regional cheese, and freshly ground coffee.

## Frequently asked questions about Venecia Antioquia

**How far is Venecia Antioquia from Medellín?**
68 km, about two hours by car.

**Is Venecia Antioquia worth visiting?**
Yes. Tourism here is still niche: less crowded than Jardín or Guatapé, but with a character those towns have started to lose.

**Can you climb Cerro Tusa without a guide?**
Technically yes, but it is not recommended. The route is not clearly marked and there are risky stretches. The guided tour with Al Parche Venecia includes insurance, equipment, and knowledge of the mountain.

**What is the best time to visit Venecia Antioquia?**
December to March and July to August. But the town shows its best face on cloudy days, when the mist between the mountains gives it an air that the sun cannot.
